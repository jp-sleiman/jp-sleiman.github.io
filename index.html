AboutPublications

I’m a Research Scientist at Flexion Robotics, where I develop whole-body controllers for humanoid robots and use them as the foundation for loco-manipulation capabilities. Previously, I spent two years at the Robotics and AI Institute (RAI) and received my PhD from the Robotic Systems Lab at ETH Zurich, advised by Prof. Marco Hutter.

My research focuses on how humanoids can quickly learn new loco-manipulation skills in simulation and transfer them reliably and robustly to the real world.
